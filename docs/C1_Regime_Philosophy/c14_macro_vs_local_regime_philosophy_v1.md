# C1.4 Macro vs Local Regime Philosophy v1

## Purpose

This document defines the relationship between Macro Regimes and Local Regimes.

---

# Core Principle

Macro and Local Regimes represent different layers of the same market system.

Neither replaces the other.

Both are required.

---

# Macro Regime

Macro Regimes describe the broader market cycle.

Primary Geographies:

* CBSA
* County

Examples:

* Washington Metro
* Los Angeles Metro
* Montgomery County

Macro Regimes capture:

* economic conditions
* housing supply conditions
* capital market conditions
* affordability conditions

at a broader market level.

---

# Local Regime

Local Regimes describe specific segments operating inside a broader market.

Examples:

* ZIP Codes
* Neighborhoods
* Property Types

Local Regimes capture local variation that may not be visible at the broader market level.

---

# Regime Divergence

Macro and Local Regimes may disagree.

Examples:

Washington Metro:
Expansion

Deanwood:
Recovery

or

Washington Metro:
Late Expansion

Luxury Condos:
Hypersupply

These situations are expected rather than exceptional.

---

# Hierarchical Interpretation

Macro Regime should generally be interpreted as the broader environment.

Local Regime should be interpreted as the specific opportunity set.

Conceptually:

Macro Regime = Wind

Local Regime = Position

Both matter.

---

# Decision Framework

Example:

Macro Expansion
+
Local Recovery

may represent a favorable opportunity.

Conversely:

Macro Recession
+
Local Expansion

may indicate elevated risk.

The platform should ultimately support both views simultaneously.

---

# Future Direction

Future versions may explicitly score:

* alignment
* divergence
* convergence

between Macro and Local Regimes.
