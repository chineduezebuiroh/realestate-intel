# C1.3 Transition Philosophy v1

## Purpose

This document defines how markets move through regimes over time.

---

# Core Principle

Markets move continuously.

Regimes are discrete labels imposed on a continuous process.

This distinction is foundational to the platform.

---

# Continuous Coordinates

Every market occupies a position on the cycle wheel.

Example:

Demand Axis = 0.72

Supply Axis = -0.31

These coordinates exist independently of any regime label.

---

# Discrete Regimes

Regimes are classifications applied to coordinates.

Examples:

* Recovery
* Expansion
* Late Expansion
* Hypersupply
* Recession

Regimes do not drive movement.

Coordinates drive movement.

---

# Transition Mechanics

Market transitions occur when coordinates cross predefined regime boundaries.

Example:

Expansion → Late Expansion

occurs because the market moved across a boundary rather than because a new label was assigned.

---

# Current Regime & Forecast Regime

The regime itself is the product. The cycle wheel is divided into:

- 4 major quadrants
- 12 minor phases

Once classified:

```text
Recovery → Early Expansion → Expansion → Late Expansion → Early Hypersupply ...
```

- the exact coordinate becomes less important.
- The classification already carries the nuance.

We don't actually need to know:
```text
Demand = 0.417 and Supply = -0.128
```

We need something that's actionable:
```text
Baltimore is currently in Early Expansion and forecasted to enter Expansion within 12 months.
```

Example:

```text
Current: Expansion
Forecast: Late Expansion
```

That is effectively a transition signal.

---

# Momentum Matters

Direction of travel matters.

Two markets can occupy similar coordinates but have different expected paths.

Example:

Market A:
Moving toward Expansion

Market B:
Moving toward Recession

Both may currently occupy similar locations.

Transition tracking should therefore include velocity and direction of movement.

---

# Forecasted Transitions

Forecasted transitions are generated using forecasted indicator values.

The same coordinate and classification framework should be used for both current and forecasted regimes.

No separate forecast regime framework should exist.

---

# Design Goal

Users should be able to answer:

* Where is this market?
* Where is it moving?
* How quickly is it moving?
* How likely is a transition?

using a single framework.
