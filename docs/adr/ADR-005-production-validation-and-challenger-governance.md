# ADR-005: Production Validation and Challenger Governance

## Status

Accepted

## Context

A challenger can reduce volatility while degrading turning-point recognition, economic interpretation, coverage, or regime persistence. Production promotion requires more than aggregate improvement.

## Decision

Production changes are evaluated through immutable baseline and challenger runs using a common comparison and review contract covering:

1. Run metadata and provenance
2. Artifact coverage and overlapping-key checks
3. Metric, dimension, and axis movement
4. Weighted dimension contributions
5. Axis cancellation
6. Coordinate displacement
7. Major, minor, and quadrant regime changes
8. Transition stability and dwell behavior
9. Chronological and geography-specific visual review
10. Production acceptance evidence

A challenger must be evaluated for structural fidelity, economic responsiveness, regime stability, interpretability, operational risk, coverage loss, non-target drift, and lineage integrity.

Lower volatility alone is not sufficient. A challenger must not merely suppress real shocks or shift classifications through unexplained lag.

County-level review is the current calibration target. Washington, DC county is mandatory. CBSA results remain in aggregate coverage but should not dominate calibration until CBSA labor and income architecture is validated.

## Consequences

- Comparison artifacts are persisted and reusable.
- Visual review is required.
- Major, minor, and micro transition behavior should be inspectable.
- Promotion decisions require ADR and registry updates.
